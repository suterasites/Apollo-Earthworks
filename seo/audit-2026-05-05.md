# Apollo Earthworks - SEO Audit (2026-05-05)

Methodology: `SEO_brief/on-page-seo.md` and `CRM/operations/sops/seo-optimisation.md`. Reference template page: `earthworks-melbourne/index.html` (city LP - already fully equipped).

## Scope

12 pages: `index`, `about`, `services`, `earthworks`, `demolition`, `civil`, `haulage-hire`, `our-work`, `contact`, `capability-statement`, `quote-template`, `thank-you`, plus `earthworks-melbourne/`.

## Critical findings

1. **Canonicals point to staging domain.** Most pages canonicalise to `https://apollo-earthworks.pages.dev/...` instead of production `https://www.apolloearthworks.com.au/...`. This actively suppresses organic ranking for the production domain. Fix on every page.
2. **Homepage missing canonical entirely.** No `<link rel="canonical">` on index.html.
3. **No Twitter cards on any page** except earthworks-melbourne LP. Lost share preview surface.
4. **No geo meta** on any page except earthworks-melbourne LP.
5. **No Open Graph image** on most pages (homepage, about, services, all 4 service pages, contact, capability-statement). Social shares render with no preview image.
6. **No BreadcrumbList JSON-LD** on most inner pages. Only contact, our-work, earthworks-melbourne have it.
7. **No skip-to-content link** on any page except earthworks-melbourne. Accessibility miss.
8. **No `<main id="main">` wrapper** on any page except earthworks-melbourne. Semantic + a11y miss.
9. **Service pages have flat Service schema** with provider as nested LocalBusiness rather than `@graph` reference. Functional but inferior - no cross-page entity reuse.
10. **No FAQPage schema** on any page except earthworks-melbourne, even though several pages have FAQ-style content (homepage, services, contact has it already).
11. **`quote-template.html` has no SEO meta and is not in sitemap.** Should be `noindex` to avoid accidental discovery.
