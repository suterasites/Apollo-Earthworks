01 - Earthworks

Bulk earthworks &
precision excavation.

Site cuts, basements, footings, trenching and final trim. Tight to spec, on schedule, and delivered with the gear and operators to back it up.

Overview

Earthworks done to the millimetre.

Earthworks is the foundation of every build. Get it wrong and every trade after you pays for it. Apollo Earthworks runs experienced operators on modern machines so the cut comes out clean, the levels are right, and the next crew can start work without a delay.

We handle the full range, from a single residential footing dig through to bulk earthworks on commercial and civil sites. From 1.7T to 30T excavators with the attachments to match the job, we bring the right gear for what your site actually needs.

Every job is run to spec, set out properly, and finished clean. That is the Apollo standard.

Services in Detail

Six core earthworks services

Each service is delivered by ticketed operators on serviced machinery, fully insured and finished to spec.

01

Bulk Earthworks

High-volume excavation and material movement for subdivisions, commercial pads and large civil works. We bring the fleet and operators to keep your program running.

  • •Subdivisions and developments
  • •Commercial pad preparation
  • •Civil and infrastructure
02

Site Cuts

Precise residential and commercial site cuts to engineer's spec. We set out, cut to level, and leave a clean pad ready for the next trade.

  • •Residential housing pads
  • •Garage and shed cuts
  • •Commercial slab prep
03

Basements

Deep excavations for basement builds. We work with engineers and shoring contractors to deliver a clean, accurate dig that supports the design.

  • •Residential basements
  • •Commercial undercrofts
  • •Carpark excavation
04

Footings

Footing excavation for slabs, walls and structural pads. Clean, accurate digs that follow the engineer's drawings to the millimetre.

  • •Strip and pad footings
  • •Stump holes and piers
  • •Retaining wall footings
05

Trenching

Service trenching for plumbing, electrical, drainage and stormwater. Tight, accurate, and dug with the right machine for the access.

  • •Stormwater and sewer
  • •Power and comms
  • •Water main runs
06

Final Trim

Precision finishing of pads, driveways and slabs to spec. We bring the bucket work and the operator skill to land you on the right level, every time.

  • •Slab and pad trim
  • •Driveway preparation
  • •Landscape final levels
