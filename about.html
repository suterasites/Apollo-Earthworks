Our Story

A Melbourne earthworks team that shows up.

Apollo Earthworks was founded by Kosta Mastelidys, a Melbourne local who built the business on a simple idea - that earthworks should be done properly, by people who turn up when they say they will and care about the result. From the first job to the latest, that has not changed.

We are based in Mount Waverley and run a modern fleet of excavators, tipper trucks, skid steers and attachments across the greater Melbourne metropolitan area. Whether it is a tight backyard pool dig, a multi-stage subdivision, or ongoing wet hire on a commercial site, we bring the right machine, the right operator and the right attitude.

The work we do is rarely glamorous. Site cuts, basements, footings, drainage, rock breaking, retaining walls, demolition and material haulage. But it is the foundation that every build sits on, and we treat it that way. Our reputation is built on long-term relationships with builders, developers and contractors who keep coming back because we deliver on time and on budget.

"Tough work. Done right. That is the only standard we know."

Kosta Mastelidys, Founder
