What We Do

Full-service earthworks for Melbourne builders and homeowners.

From a single-day residential site cut to multi-week commercial excavation, Apollo Earthworks handles the full scope. Every job is quoted fixed, every machine is operated by a ticketed operator, and every site is left clean and ready for the next trade.

Site Cuts

Questions

Frequently asked.

What areas of Melbourne do you service for earthworks? +

Apollo Earthworks services Eastern Melbourne, South East Melbourne, and the Mornington Peninsula. Our yard is based in Mount Waverley and we travel throughout the eastern and south eastern suburbs daily.

How much does a site cut cost in Melbourne? +

Site cut pricing depends on the volume of material to be removed, site access, soil type, and whether tipping is included. Most residential site cuts fall between $3,000 and $15,000. We provide fixed quotes after a free on-site assessment so there are no surprises.

How long does a typical earthworks job take? +

A standard residential site cut usually takes one to three days. Bulk excavation for basements or larger commercial sites can take one to three weeks. We confirm exact timelines with each quote and commit to the dates we book.

Do you supply your own machinery and operators? +

Yes. We run a modern fleet of excavators from 1.7T to 30T, skid steers, tandem tippers, rock breakers, and compaction equipment. Every machine on site is operated by a ticketed, experienced operator.

Do you work on residential and commercial projects? +

Both. We work with owner builders, residential builders, developers, civil contractors, concreters, landscapers, and plumbers across residential, commercial, and civil projects. No job is too small or too large.

Are you fully insured? +

Yes. Apollo Earthworks is fully insured, OH&S compliant, and all operators hold current White Card and machine tickets. SWMS documentation is available on request for every site.

Can I hire an excavator with an operator (wet hire)? +

Yes. We offer excavator and Posi-Track wet hire by the day or week, including operator, fuel, and transport. This suits builders and contractors who need short-term capacity without running their own fleet.

How quickly can you start? +

For most residential jobs we can attend site for a quote within 24 to 48 hours and book works within one to two weeks of acceptance. Urgent jobs can often be accommodated sooner. Call 0431 560 908 to check current availability.
