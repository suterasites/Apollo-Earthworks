About Us

Hard Work, Precision, Reliability

Apollo Earthworks is a Melbourne-based earthworks company offering excavation and civil services across residential, industrial, commercial and civil sectors. We cover the full spectrum of earthworks, from residential site prep through to commercial and civil projects. Our wet hire service caters to both DIY/builder clients and larger contractors.
