Fully insured, SWMS available, OH&S compliant. We turn up, do the job safely, and clear out so the next trade can start.

Process

How Apollo runs a demolition or site-clearance job

Same six-step approach whether we are pulling down a single shed or clearing a residential block for a new build. Safe, permitted, and finished clean.

01

Site walk and scope

We walk the site within 24 to 48 hours, identify the structures or vegetation coming down, flag any asbestos, services, or boundary issues, and confirm the disposal plan. Fixed quote follows.

02

Permits and disconnections

We will not pull the trigger on any demo until permits are sighted, asbestos clearance is in hand if relevant, and power, gas, water and telco are properly disconnected. We point you to the right surveyor and utilities.

03

Set-up and safety

On day one we install temporary fencing or barriers, position the float and machine, run a Dial Before You Dig check, and walk through the SWMS with the crew. Adjacent properties are protected, exclusion zones marked.

04

Demolition or clearance

The excavator works the structure down or strips the block of vegetation, slabs and rubble. Rock breaker attached for hard concrete or rock seams. Spoil and rubble feed direct to the tandem tipper for cartage off-site.

05

Material separation and disposal

Concrete, brick and steel separated where practical so it can go to recycling rather than landfill. Clean fill, green waste and mixed waste all routed to the right facility. Tipping receipts kept on file.

06

Site clean and handover

Block left ready for the next stage, fences and barriers removed, footpaths swept, and any council reinstatement noted. You walk onto a clean site, not a debris field.

Cost factors

What affects demolition cost in Melbourne?

Demolition pricing across Melbourne can range from $1,500 for a small shed or fence right through to $25,000+ for a full single-storey house pull-down. The factors below explain where your job sits in that band.

Every quote from Apollo is fixed and itemised so the line items are visible upfront.

Structure size and material

A timber-frame carport pulls down faster and cheaper than a double-brick garage. Reinforced concrete slabs, brick veneer houses, and steel-framed sheds all sit at different points on the time-and-machine curve.

Asbestos and hazardous materials

Asbestos sheeting, eaves, or floor tiles must be removed by a licensed removalist before our crew starts. The cost of that scope is separate from the demolition itself and reflects the disposal pathway it requires.

Access and adjacent property

Open access for a 20T excavator and tipper is the cheapest scenario. Heritage neighbours, party walls, or zero-lot-line builds need slower, more careful work plus temporary protection. We quote the protection in.

Disposal volume and recycling

Cubic metres of waste cart-off, recycling fees, and tip-site distance all factor in. Concrete and brick can often be recycled cheaper than going to landfill, which we plan for where the structure allows.

Permits and reinstatement

Building permits, council Report and Consent, footpath reinstatement, and crossover repairs are all itemised separately. We flag what your job needs at quote stage so there are no surprises after demolition is complete.

FAQ

Demolition Melbourne, common questions

How much does demolition cost in Melbourne?

+

Small structure demolition (sheds, carports, brick fences, single garages) typically falls between $1,500 and $6,000. Full single-storey house demolition usually runs $15,000 to $25,000 once asbestos handling, disconnections, and tipping are factored in. Apollo quotes each job fixed after a site walk-through so the number stands.

Do you handle asbestos?

+

We do not remove asbestos in-house. If asbestos is identified during quoting we coordinate a licensed removalist before our demolition crew starts, so the site is clean and compliant when our machines arrive. Clearance certificates are kept on file.

Do I need a permit to demolish a structure?

+

Most demolition works in Victoria require a Building Permit and may also need council Report and Consent. Apollo can advise on what your job needs and can refer a building surveyor if you do not have one. Permits are the owner's responsibility, but we will not start work without sighting them.

Can you break out rock and concrete?

+

Yes. We run rock breaker hammer attachments across the fleet for hard rock, basalt, and reinforced concrete. The hammer is more cost-effective than chasing the same job with bucket teeth, especially on slabs, footings, and rock seams in the dig area.

Do you remove the cleared material from site?

+

Yes. Our tandem tippers cart the broken material, soil, vegetation, and rubbish to the nearest accepting tip or recycling facility. Tipping fees are itemised on every quote so you see the disposal cost upfront.

How long does a small demolition take?

+

Shed, carport, or fence demolition usually runs a half to a full day on site. Pool demolitions take one to two days depending on size and access. Full house demolition is typically a two to four day program once disconnections and asbestos clearance are complete.

What do I need to do before you start?

+

Disconnect power, gas, water, and any telco services to the structure (we can point you to the right utilities). Clear belongings from the building. Provide site access and any required permits. Apollo handles set-out, safety control, demo, and clean-up from there.
