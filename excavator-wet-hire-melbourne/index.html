The small end of the fleet goes where bigger gear cannot: down the side of a house, into a backyard, around a pool or an extension. Trenching for plumbers and electricians, footings for decks and pergolas, and small site cuts.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How much does excavator wet hire cost in Melbourne? +

It comes down to three things: the size of the machine, how long you book it, and which attachments go with it. A mini for a single day is the smallest booking and a 30T machine for a week is the largest. Every rate we give includes the operator, fuel and the float to and from site, so none of the basics turn up later as extras. Call 0431 560 908 with the machine size and dates for a rate.

Is the operator included in excavator hire? +

Yes, on every booking. Our excavators only go out with one of our own operators, never dry hired. Your supervisor sets the work for the day and our operator runs the machine to it, so you are not paying for someone unfamiliar with the machine to find their feet on your site.

What size excavator do I need? +

Size it to the access first and the volume second. A compact machine from 1.7T to 5T fits through side gates for backyard trenching, footings and pool digs. The 8T to 20T range handles house site cuts, civil works and commercial jobs. Machines from 20T to 30T are for bulk excavation and basements. Tell us the job and the access, and we will put the right machine on it, because a machine that is too big for the site only costs you more per hour.

Can I hire an excavator for just one day? +

Yes. One-day bookings are common for a trench, a footing dig or a tidy-up before the next trade. The float to site and back costs the same whether the machine stays for one day or five, so if the work might stretch to two or three days it usually works out cheaper to book the days together than to bring the machine back later.

Do you hire mini excavators for backyards and tight access? +

Yes. Our compact machines start at 1.7T, which is small enough to fit down the side of most houses and work in a backyard without taking out a fence. They suit plumbers, landscapers, pool builders and owner-builders who need a small dig done properly by someone who knows the machine.

What do you need from me before the machine arrives? +

The site address, a spot where the machine can be unloaded, the pegs or levels it will dig to, and the location of any services you know about. Our operator arrives with a SWMS, holds a White Card and the machine tickets, and will complete your site induction if you run one.

Can you take the spoil away as well? +

Yes. Add tandem tippers with drivers to the booking and the trucks cycle between the excavator and the tip while the machine keeps digging. Tip fees are charged at what the tip charges us, and the tip receipts come with the invoice.

Ready to dig in?

Machine size, dates and the site address. That is all we need to come back with a day or weekly rate.
