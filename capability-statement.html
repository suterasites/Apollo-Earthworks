Company Overview

Apollo Earthworks is a Melbourne-based earthmoving and haulage company providing reliable excavation, bulk earthworks, site cuts, retaining walls, and material haulage services across residential, commercial, and civil projects. We pride ourselves on being dependable, efficient, and easy to work with, delivering projects on time and on budget.

Our focus is building long-term relationships with builders, developers, and contractors by providing consistent service, experienced operators, and well-maintained equipment.

Our Approach

At Apollo Earthworks, every project starts with a clear understanding of your requirements. We work closely with our clients from the initial consultation through to project completion, ensuring open communication, accurate timelines, and no surprises. Whether it is a single-lot residential site cut or a multi-stage commercial development, we bring the same level of professionalism and commitment to every job.

We believe that reliable earthworks form the foundation of every successful build. That is why we invest in modern equipment, experienced operators, and efficient processes - so our clients can move forward with confidence.
