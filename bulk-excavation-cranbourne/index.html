Questions

Frequently asked.

What counts as bulk excavation? +

Bulk excavation is any large-volume dig where the volume of material, not the fine detail, drives the job: basements, pools, subdivision cut-to-fill, deep site cuts and big level changes. The measure is cubic metres moved and truck loads carted out. Smaller trenching, footings and final trim work sit under general earthworks.

How much does bulk excavation cost in Cranbourne? +

Bulk excavation is priced on volume, access, soil type and where the spoil goes. Cartage and tipping are usually the biggest single line, not the digging, so the classification of the material matters more than the hole. Most Cranbourne residential bulk digs land between $8,000 and $40,000. We quote fixed after a free on-site assessment.

How is the spoil handled and where does it go? +

We cart with our own tandem tippers, so the dig and the export are on one timeline rather than waiting on a subcontracted truck. Material is classified before it leaves and taken to a facility licensed to receive that classification. Clean fill can often go to another site, which brings the tipping cost down.

What size machines do you bring to a Cranbourne bulk dig? +

Bulk work generally runs 20T to 30T excavators paired with the tipper fleet, which is what makes the volume move in days rather than weeks. Where access is tight we stage it: a smaller machine opens the access and the larger machine follows once there is room to work.

What if you hit rock? +

We carry rock breakers and attachments, so rock is a variation to the program rather than a stop. On the site assessment we look at what neighbouring sites turned up and flag the risk before quoting, so a rock clause is priced in rather than sprung on you halfway through.

Can you do bulk excavation on a subdivision or estate site? +

Yes. Subdivision and estate bulk earth, cut-to-fill across multiple lots and staged bulk digs are core work for us in the Cranbourne growth corridor. We work to the civil drawings and to the builder's program, staging the export so the site stays workable for the trades behind us.

How long does a bulk excavation take? +

A residential basement or pool dig is usually two to five days including cartage. Subdivision and estate bulk earth is quoted per stage and can run weeks, driven by the daily truck movements the site and the roads can actually take. We lock the timeline at quote stage.
